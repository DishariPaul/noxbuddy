A study day

In four little steps.

Each companion has its own quiet role to play throughout your day.

01 — Plan it Planr

Write down what matters today.

No endless backlog. Just a clean, notebook-style sheet to capture what you actually intend to finish before turning off the lamp.

✓ Finish DBMS chapter 4
Revise dynamic programming
Push portfolio updates
02 — Focus on it Pawmo

Set a timer, start a session and keep your streak alive.

Pawmo sits quietly by your timer. Every completed session earns steady energy, gentle milestones, and uninterrupted deep focus.

25:00 🐱 Ready to focus · 7-day streak
03 — Ask when you're stuck Pixel

Talk through questions, learn something new or simply have a conversation.

A friendly companion when concepts feel difficult or placement anxiety kicks in. Pixel explains simply, quizzes patiently, and never judges.

Can we break down binary search trees?
Think of it like an organized bookshelf: smaller numbers on your left, larger on your right. Let's trace one example together.
04 — Leave something behind Pebble

Write about your day and check in with yourself.

A gentle mood-adaptive journal. Pick how you feel, write down an honest paragraph about the day, and let your study desk reset for tomorrow.
